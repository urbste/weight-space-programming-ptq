I changed the quantized network so the ternary codes and scales match the float weights much better. The RTN network sets the scale to the row's maximum weight and zeroes every weight below half of it, so it drops most weights and overstates the rest. I could not run anything, so none of this is tested.

- **Scales:** each row's scale is now 0.55 times the RTN scale, close to the mean magnitude of the weights that stay nonzero. In conv4 I only rescaled, by 0.72.
- **conv1, conv2 and fc:** every weight with magnitude at least 0.32 of the row maximum now gets a code of plus or minus 1. In fc this was done with `R` lines.
- **conv3:** I kept all RTN nonzeros and added codes for weights with magnitude about 0.33 to 0.5 of the row maximum. I scanned all 32 rows by hand, so a few borderline weights may be missed.
- **conv4 codes and all biases:** unchanged. conv4 has about 14k weights and the edits did not fit in one response.
